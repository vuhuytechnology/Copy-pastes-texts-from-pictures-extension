Select & Copy Text from Images

Settings saved

Languages other than English/Vietnamese are downloaded on first use (requires internet) and cached locally.

Quick Guide

  • Hover: Move over any image and click Select Text at the top-right corner.
  • Right-click: Right-click an image and choose Select text on this image (OCR).
  • Region: Press Alt+Shift+O and drag to capture text from videos, canvas, or backgrounds.
  • Copy: Highlight text directly on the image and press Ctrl+C, or click Copy All.
